Loiter Mode & Pastlight

This site hosts the information page for heph-content, a private upload tool used by the owner of two YouTube channels:

  • Loiter Mode: fixed-wing FPV flight.
  • Pastlight: history and technology documentaries.

The tool uses YouTube API Services to publish videos and their titles, descriptions and thumbnails to those channels on the owner's behalf. Uploads are private unless the owner chooses otherwise. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
