Privacy policy

Effective 23 September 2026

heph-content is a private tool used by the owner of the Loiter Mode and Pastlight YouTube channels to upload and manage their own videos. It has one user: the channel owner.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions and schedules, and read basic channel information (channel name and ID) for the owner's own channels only.

What it stores

It stores the OAuth access credentials Google issues, encrypted, on the owner's own private infrastructure. It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of anyone else, and it does not read viewers' data.

Sharing

No data is shared with third parties. The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google and YouTube terms

Use of YouTube through this tool is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
